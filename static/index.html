Real-Time 2nd Primary Molar & 2nd Premolar Segmentation, Contour Width & Interactive Contact Point Adjustment

Stage 1
Data Collection & Preprocessing
Panoramic OPGs, CLAHE contrast enhancement & bilateral denoising; 70/15/15 Train/Val/Test split
Stage 2
Annotation & Binary Masks
2nd Primary Molar & 2nd Premolar annotated into white (255) tooth masks on black (0) background
Stage 3
U-Net Selection & Training
CoordConv U-Net trained with pixel-wise Cross-Entropy + Dice loss & spatial/contrast augmentations
Stage 4
Segmentation & Width Measurement
Contour extraction identifies leftmost & rightmost contact points; interactive drag recalculates live
Stage 5
Conversion to Real-World Units
Scale marker / DPI pixel-to-mm calibration computes MDW & E-Space (Molar MDW − Premolar MDW) in mm
